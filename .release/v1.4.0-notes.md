## What's new in 1.4.0

**Plan mode.** Turn it on for any month that isn't fully in the past — the current month or any future one — and tap ahead to sketch out a schedule: Present, Work from home, Leave, or Comp off, day by day, exactly like logging a real day. Planned days show up as dashed, hollow cells so they never get mistaken for something you've actually logged.

**A projected percentage, separate from your real one.** Once you've planned a few days, a new "Projected" figure appears showing where your financial year lands if you follow that plan — purely a forecast. It never touches your real attendance percentage; that only ever moves when you log a day for real, the same as before.

**Turning a month's plan off asks first.** Flip Plan mode off for a month with days already planned and it'll confirm before clearing them — a plan you spent time building can't disappear from an accidental tap.
