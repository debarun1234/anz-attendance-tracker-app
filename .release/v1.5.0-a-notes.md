## What's new in 1.5.0-a

**Office auto-detection.** Add your office locations under Settings → Location, and when you're nearby, Today shows a banner offering to mark you present — you always confirm it yourself with one tap; nothing gets logged automatically. The two Bangalore offices, Acacia and Hibiscus, come pre-loaded as a starting point.

**Built-in safety.** It never fires on weekends, and it never overrides a day you've already logged — a match is only ever offered on an unmarked weekday, once. It's off by default; turn it on from Settings when you want it.

**Split time between offices, or somewhere else entirely?** Every saved office is checked each time, so nothing is fixed to one location — add as many as you use. On a day that matches none of them (a client site, a third office, working from somewhere else), the app doesn't stay quiet: it nudges you to log the day yourself instead of letting it slip through unmarked.

**A full-screen reveal when it's confirmed.** Confirm a GPS match and a one-time "Present — logged" animation plays — a stamp landing with a burst of colour, your office name, and the date. It also catches up if you don't open the app again until later: check in at 9am, come back at 9pm, and it still plays once, then. Works the same in the installed app and the browser version, and fires even when the app is brought forward from the Android recent-apps switcher, not just on a fresh launch.

**A dedicated locations page.** Manage office locations from their own page off Settings: add, edit, or remove one, and use "Use my current location" while you're standing at the office for the most accurate reading — the pre-loaded coordinates are business-park-level approximations meant to be refined this way.

**Daily reminders.** Set a morning and end-of-day time and Office Ledger nudges you to log today — built for days working from home, so nothing slips through unlogged. Android only, same limitation as every other notification-centre push in this app: a closed browser tab on iPhone or the web app can't fire one on its own.

**Plan mode, sharper numbers.** The Month Summary card now shows a Projected figure for the month you're actively planning, not just the financial-year total. And the recovery message no longer averages the days you need evenly across every remaining calendar month — it looks up exactly how many eligible days are left in each one, so a nearly-finished month doesn't get credited with runway it doesn't have.

**On-device, same as everything else.** Location is compared against your saved offices entirely on this phone. Nothing about where you are is ever sent anywhere.
