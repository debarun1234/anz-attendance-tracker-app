## What's new in 1.5.3

**Fixed: the location permission prompt.** On the Android app, office auto-detection's underlying Geolocation plugin had never actually been wired into the native build — it silently fell back to a path that doesn't reliably trigger Android's permission dialog, so turning the feature on could go nowhere. That's fixed from this version: the permission prompt now fires properly the first time you enable it.

**A live GPS status in Settings.** Turn on auto-detect and Settings now shows exactly where things stand — permission granted or not, GPS acquired or not, and how far the last reading was from your nearest saved office — refreshed automatically when you open the page, or on demand with "Check GPS now." It's a point-in-time check, not a running tracker, in keeping with the app never watching location in the background.

**Notification permission, made explicit.** Declared directly in the app now rather than relying only on the notifications plugin to bring it in on its own.
