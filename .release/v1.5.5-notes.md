## What's new in 1.5.5

**Auto-confirm, opt-in.** Office auto-detection has always asked you to confirm a match with one tap before logging it — a deliberate default, since "near the office" isn't always "at work." Settings → Location now has a toggle for anyone who'd rather skip that: turn on auto-confirm and a match logs the day present the instant it's found, no tap, no banner — the celebration reveal is your only sign it happened. Every other safeguard (weekdays only, never overriding an already-logged day, once a day) applies exactly the same either way. Off by default; the confirm-first flow is unchanged unless you turn it on.
