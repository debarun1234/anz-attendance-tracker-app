## What's new in 1.5.6

**Fixed: update notifications on Android could go silent for good.** If the OS notification push for "an update is available" ever failed once — most commonly because notification permission hadn't been granted the very first time it silently checked — it was marked as handled and never tried again, from either the silent background check or a manual check in Settings. The on-screen "Version X available" text in Settings always kept working (it's a separate code path), which is why it looked like only the manual check was reliable. Now the two are tracked separately: the app still avoids stacking up duplicate rows in your notification inbox, but the actual push to your phone's notification centre keeps retrying on every check until it actually succeeds.
