## What's new in 1.5.7

**Fixed: Attendance % could read too high, most visible when WFH days made up a big share of the month.** The app infers extra "leave taken" days when your reported leave balance drops by more than what you've explicitly marked as Leave on the Calendar — useful when leave gets taken and the balance updated without a matching tap on a day. But it wasn't checking whether there was actually a genuinely-unmarked day to explain that drop: if every weekday that month was already accounted for (Present, WFH, or an explicit Leave mark), it would still shave the inferred amount off Eligible working days without touching Attended — quietly inflating the percentage, and in some cases making Eligible read lower than Attended plus WFH, which never makes sense. It now only ever borrows from days that are genuinely still unmarked, and never more of them than actually exist. Every past month recalculates automatically — nothing to redo.
