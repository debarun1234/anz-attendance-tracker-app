01

Why this exists

ANZ's hybrid‑work policy runs on a percentage. Most of us track that percentage the way we track our steps: badly, from memory, right before the quarterly check‑in — usually while squinting at a spreadsheet someone built in 2019.

This doesn't do that. It's a small, on‑device calculator that logs your days as you go, does the actual maths the policy is built on, and tells you — plainly, without HR euphemisms — exactly how many more office days you need this month.

Built after one too many Sunday‑night reconciliations. No login. No backend. No one but you ever sees the numbers.

The actual formula
attendance % = days present ÷ eligible working days × 100
eligible days = working days − leave − holidays − comp‑off
Same formula the app runs on your phone. No hidden multiplier, no vibes‑based rounding.

What's on the ledger

01

Calendar tracking

Tap any day — Present, Work from home, Leave, Comp off, or a worked weekend. Colour‑coded, so a whole month reads at a glance instead of a wall of blue.

02

Two leave pools, tracked honestly

Annual and Sick/Carer draw from separate balances — because HR already keeps them separate, the app just stopped pretending otherwise.

03

FY aggregate, month by month

Every month since 1 October, plus the running total, so "how am I tracking this year" finally has a real answer.

04

Recovery maths

Below target? It tells you exactly how many more days, out of how many are left, to get back on track — no mental arithmetic required on a Friday afternoon.

05

Notification centre

App errors, update alerts, and a receipt every time you send a bug report — one bell icon, not three toast messages you never saw.

06

Nothing leaves your phone

No login, no backend, no server to breach. Your attendance history is exactly as private as your actual attendance.

Android is warning me about "unknown sources." Is this safe?

Yes — that warning appears for any app installed outside the Play Store, including this one. Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps → allow it for your browser or Files app, then install as normal. The app makes no network calls except an optional, anonymous check for new GitHub releases.

Does this connect to any ANZ system?

No. It doesn't touch SuccessFactors, your calendar, badge data, or anything else. You tell it what happened; it does the maths. That's the entire integration surface.

Found a bug?

Report it from inside the app (Settings → About → Report a bug) or open an issue on GitHub. Both reach the same person, at the same speed.
